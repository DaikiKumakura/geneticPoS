# geneticPoS 0.5.0

- `karim_pos()` accepts, through `links`, the MR-to-coloc row correspondences that you have confirmed from papers or analysis records, together with their evidence.
- Known MR and coloc column names and supplementary metadata are used to retrieve the matching Karim benchmark from the bundled reference.
- The package ships the steps of an independent check on real pQTL data and a script that reruns its application to the support group.
